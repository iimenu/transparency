Correspondence and documentation relating to legal notices received by the operators of iistupid.com and github.com/iireborn.

## Summary

25 September 2026, two notices from Grayson (crimsoncauldron), Administrator, Goldentrophy Software:

1. [Notice of GNU GPL v3 Violation](25_09_26_grayson/original_Notice%20of%20GNU%20GPL%20V3%20Violation.docx.md) — alleging license violations concerning ii's Stupid Menu
2. [Cease and Desist](25_09_26_grayson/original_Unauthorized%20Use%20of%20the%20iiDk%20and%20ii's%20Stupid%20Menu%20Marks%20and%20Copyrighted%20Artwork.md) — alleging trademark and copyright claims

The explicit directive to "DO NOT REDISTRIBUTE" on both notices is legally unenforceable for documents of this nature and has therefore been ignored. Per 17 U.S.C. § 107 (Fair Use), publishing it for criticism, comment, and news reporting is permitted. In *Bartnicki v. Vopper* (2001), the Supreme Court held that publishing truthful information on matters of public concern receives First Amendment protection, even if the information was obtained by questionable means. A legal threat sent to multiple operators is a valid matter of public concern to their community. Copyright never protects facts, legal arguments, or ideas, only the specific creative expression of them.

A single response addressing both notices was sent on September 25th, 2026 and is published here: [response_001.md](25_09_26_grayson/response_corgi_001.md)

No reply has been acknowledged so far as of your reading of this repository.
